How it plays

Three pieces. One board.

Easy to read, hard to put down — every move is one decision that matters.

01

Drag pieces in

Three pieces wait in the tray. Drop each one where it fits on the 8×8 board. No rotation — placement is the whole game.

02

Fill a line

Complete a whole row or column and it clears with a radial shatter, opening up space for your next move.

03

Ride the multiplier

Clear on back-to-back moves and the multiplier climbs — there's no ceiling, so a hot streak can snowball into a massive run.
