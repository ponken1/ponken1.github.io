Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Version note: The separate analytics-sharing control and expanded Pro benefits described below apply to version 1.2.3. Earlier versions do not include that separate analytics control; their analytics may also use persistent device identifiers. Version 1.2.3 is being prepared for App Store release.

Gridblitz (“the app”, “we”) is a block-placement puzzle game for iOS. This policy explains what information the app and the services it uses handle. You can play without an account, analytics sharing, or watching ads.

Your choices: gameplay analytics sharing is off until you enable it in Settings. Optional rewarded ads provide one revive per run and selected cosmetic unlocks. These services can process device identifiers; having no account does not make their data anonymous.

Game progress and purchases

The app has no account, login, or sign-up. Your saved board, best scores, settings and unlocked skins are stored on your device. High scores may also be sent to Game Center, and gameplay scores and unlock events may be sent to GameAnalytics when you choose to share analytics.

Gridblitz Pro is an optional purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks your purchase entitlement to unlock every block and board skin and provide one ad-free revive per run. Pro owners do not need to watch ads for skins. We do not receive your payment-card details. If analytics sharing is enabled, the app sends a purchase-completed event to GameAnalytics; it does not send a receipt, transaction identifier or payment details to that service.

Game Center (leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your high scores are submitted to the Gridblitz leaderboards so you can compare with others. Game Center is an Apple service governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We only send a score value and the associated Game Center identifier Apple provides; we do not receive your Apple ID, name, or email.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Optional rewarded ads are available for one revive per run and selected cosmetic unlocks. The game is fully playable without ever watching an ad. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), approximate/general location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data. With your permission, this may be used for personalized advertising.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics & diagnostics (GameAnalytics)

Sharing is off by default. You can enable “Share gameplay analytics” in the app's Settings to help us understand play and purchases. If enabled, the app sends mode choices, scores, revive and cosmetic events, paywall views and purchase outcomes to GameAnalytics. Its SDK also processes session, device and diagnostic information. Events are linked to a persistent device identifier (IDFV, used as the SDK's user identifier), and may include the advertising identifier (IDFA) when iOS tracking permission allows it. This is pseudonymous data, not anonymous data.

Turning sharing off stops new analytics event submission and local event caching, including SDK session and diagnostic events. Requests already in flight may finish, and SDK configuration requests may continue until the app is restarted. On the next launch, GameAnalytics is not initialized while sharing remains off. Turning sharing off does not delete previously received data; contact us for a data request.

Advertising and tracking choices

Google's consent flow runs before the ad SDK starts. Where required, it asks for your ad privacy choices; you can revisit these through “Ad privacy options” in the app's Settings. Analytics sharing is a separate choice and is not enabled by agreeing to ads.

iOS also asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before the advertising identifier is used. Declining tracking does not limit gameplay. Optional ad rewards remain available when Google can serve an eligible ad; availability can depend on consent choices and network fill. You can change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Gridblitz is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The data processing is the analytics and optional advertising described above.

Data we share or sell

We do not sell your personal data. The services described here are Apple (Game Center and purchases), GameAnalytics (analytics & diagnostics) and Google (AdMob), as described above, for the sole purposes of running leaderboards, improving the game, and serving the optional ads.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking at the prompt, or turn it off in iOS Settings.
  • Turn gameplay analytics sharing on or off in the app's Settings. Restart after turning it off to fully stop the analytics SDK.
  • Review ad consent through Ad privacy options in the app's Settings when available.
  • Delete the app to remove its locally stored game data. This does not delete data already received by Apple, Google or GameAnalytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this URL.
